Discussion Guidelines
Good Practices
  • Use clear, descriptive titles
  • Search for existing discussions before creating new ones
  • Keep discussions focused on course topics
  • Encourage respectful debate and diverse perspectives
  • Pin important announcements or frequently asked questions
Avoid
  • Duplicate topics or questions
  • Off-topic discussions
  • Posting personal contact information
  • Spam or promotional content
  • Disrespectful language or behavior
Moderation
  • Monitor discussions regularly
  • Address inappropriate content promptly
  • Lock discussions when they become outdated
  • Archive resolved discussions
  • Provide clear moderation guidelines
Engagement Tips
  • Ask open-ended questions to encourage discussion
  • Respond to student questions promptly
  • Share relevant resources and examples
  • Recognize and highlight valuable contributions
